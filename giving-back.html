Giving Back

Serving our community is part of who we are. A portion of every job we do goes to supporting the most vulnerable people in Southwest Florida and beyond.

Our Heart

Dedicated To Our Local Community

AJ’s Yacht Service is a division of AJ’s Marine Group, owned and operated by Adam & Rachel Jones. We believe in leveraging our gifts, passion, resources, and relentless integrity — on the water and in our community.

More Than Good Intentions — Intentional Action

Adam and Rachel believe their life’s mission is about serving and financially supporting the most vulnerable people in our communities — children in foster care, veterans, and those experiencing homelessness.

Every week, they make it a point to hit the streets, reaching out to people experiencing homelessness and providing the help they need. It’s not just about good intentions — it’s about intentional action that makes a real difference in people’s lives.

Where Your Support Goes

Organizations We Support

We donate a net percentage of every job to local and global nonprofit organizations, including these partners doing life-changing work.

World Vision

Humanitarian aid for vulnerable children and families in communities worldwide.

A21

Fighting human trafficking and walking with survivors toward freedom and restoration.
