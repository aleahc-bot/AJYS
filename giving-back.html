Giving Back

Rooted in excellence and driven by purpose. A portion of every job we do goes toward serving the most vulnerable people in our community, because every detail matters, on the vessel and beyond it.

Our Heart

Dedicated To Our Local Community

AJ’s Yacht Service is a division of AJ’s Marine Group, owned and operated by Adam and Rachel Jones. We believe in leveraging our gifts, passion, resources, and relentless integrity, both on the water and in our community.

More Than Good Intentions

Intentional Action, Every Week

Adam and Rachel believe their life’s mission is about serving and financially supporting the most vulnerable people in our communities: children in foster care, veterans, and those experiencing homelessness.

Every week, they make it a point to reach out to people experiencing homelessness and provide the help they need. It isn’t just about good intentions. It’s about intentional action that makes a real difference in people’s lives.

Where Your Support Goes
